Welcome

Hollowmere builds small, handmade websites for local businesses, makers and clubs. Each one is designed around the people who will use it, so customers find what they came for in a few seconds and remember the place afterwards.

This castle is a working demo of that idea. The sandstone bricks on the left are the menu: press one to open a room, and the stained glass behind it lights up to show where you are. The gray bricks are loose, so knock one out, try to catch it, or hold it down until it crumbles.

  • Built for your customers. Clear wording, the right information first, and a page that feels like your business.
  • Ready on every screen. Phones, tablets and desktops, checked by hand.
  • Online without the fuss. Hosting set up and your own web address connected for you.

About us

The keeper of Hollowmere builds websites one at a time, by hand, for bakers, barbers, bands and anyone else who wants a corner of the internet that feels like theirs.

How it works

  1. A short chat. You tell me what you do, who your customers are, and what they need to find.
  2. A first version. You get a live link to try on your own phone.
  3. Changes. We adjust the words, pictures and colors until it feels right.
  4. Launch. I connect your web address and set up hosting, and the site goes live.
